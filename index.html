FAQ

Questions agents ask.

Will leads know it's automated?

Cally's texts are short and friendly, and it says it's an automated assistant if anyone asks. If a lead asks for a person or replies with something Cally can't handle, it stops and texts you instead.

How long does setup take?

A few minutes. You get your Cally address right after signing up. Realtor.com has a built-in setting for it, and Zillow or website leads need one forwarding rule.

Does it work with Zillow leads?

Yes. Zillow and Realtor.com email you each new lead. Those emails go to your Cally address, and Cally replies to the lead within about a minute.

What about texting rules and opt-outs?

Every first text includes opt-out instructions, and anyone who replies STOP is never texted again. Your lead forms should also ask for consent to text. Add a line like this next to your form's submit button: “By submitting, you agree to receive texts about your inquiry from [your name]. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.”

Does Cally give advice about homes or prices?

No. Cally only confirms the inquiry, asks qualifying questions, and helps schedule a call with you. Pricing and property questions go to you.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. There's no contract. Cancel from your billing link and your service stops at the end of the month.
