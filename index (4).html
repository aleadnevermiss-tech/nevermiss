Questions

Will leads know it's automated?

Cally's texts are short and friendly and sign off with your team's name. If a lead asks for a person or replies with something Cally can't handle, it stops and texts you instead.

How long does setup take?

Most agents are live within 2–3 business days. You fill out one form and forward your lead alerts to an address we give you.

Does it work with Zillow leads?

Yes. Zillow and Realtor.com send you an email for each new lead. You forward those emails to Cally, and it replies to the lead within a minute.

What about texting rules and opt-outs?

Every text includes opt-out instructions, and anyone who replies STOP is never texted again. Your lead forms need texting-consent wording, and we'll send you the exact language to add.

Does Cally give advice about homes or prices?

No. Cally only confirms the inquiry, asks qualifying questions, and helps schedule a call with you. Pricing and property questions go to you.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. There's no contract. Reply to any billing email and we'll stop your service at the end of the month.
